# L5 — Competitive landscape, pricing, and sizing

L5 produced 48 findings from 14 distinct URLs. The evidence independently supports the
meeting's broad price band but not a public rate card: Epoch AI reports interviewees'
observed range of roughly $200–$2,000 per task, Mechanize argues for a compute-derived
floor near $500, and contributor/labour markets span roughly $75–$200 per expert hour.
Environment-level figures are materially larger and must not be mixed with per-task
prices. Published benchmark sizes cluster around 75–90 tasks for difficult curated sets,
with reusable computer-use environments supporting hundreds.

This lane is highly concentrated: 13 records come from one Epoch AI article, and most
prices are interviews, analyst estimates, forecasts, or vendor claims rather than signed
contracts. The round may use them as anchors, not as a pricing recommendation. Direct
buyer discovery remains necessary before a commercial decision.
