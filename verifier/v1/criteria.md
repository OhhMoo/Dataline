# Verifier v1 — Acceptance criteria for de-agent-benchmark-assessment.md

Created: 2026-09-08. Measures whether the deliverable answers the user's three asks.

## Checklist
1. **Feasibility evaluation present** — assesses technical, operational, and commercial
   feasibility, with named hard problems and ranked risks.
2. **Overall goal stated** — a single distilled goal plus falsifiable near/mid/long-term
   milestones, not just a restatement of the doc.
3. **Defensibility section present** — concrete, non-generic moat mechanics (what to build,
   what to keep private, what will NOT work as a moat).
4. **Value for Alkera addressed** — covers both plausible readings (Alkera-as-sponsor vs
   Alkera-as-separate-company), grounded in Alkera's actual market position.
5. **Grounding** — market/competitive claims trace to the 2026-09-08 web scan or the
   planning doc; no invented figures; the DataAgentBench ownership correction is included.
6. **Actionable** — prioritized next-actions list exists.

## Method
Manual checklist review by the lead agent against the deliverable; each item pass/fail.
Logged under verifier/runs/.
