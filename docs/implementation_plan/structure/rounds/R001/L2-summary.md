L2 produced 26 findings from 12 distinct URLs. MLE-Bench, production-ML interview
studies, leakage surveys, and first-party platform accounts supplied the strongest
evidence. The central result is that realistic ML-engineering failures usually live in
the data and operational path: leakage, training-serving skew, delayed labels, drift,
copied filters, stale notebook state, missing observability, malformed deliverables, and
poor compute budgeting. L2 also resolved the meeting transcription: “Emily Bench” is
MLE-Bench.

The source base is strong but concentrated, particularly around MLE-Bench and two
interview/survey papers. Gaps remain around feature-store incidents, experiment-tracking
failures, model-registry and deployment rollbacks, and long-horizon ML tasks that span the
