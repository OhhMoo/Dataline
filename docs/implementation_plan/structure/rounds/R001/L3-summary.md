# L3 — Analytics-engineering practitioner workflows

L3 produced 19 findings from 18 distinct URLs, the least concentrated of the three
workflow lanes. First-party engineering blogs, practitioner essays, migration guides,
and BI community records converge on metric reconciliation as the dominant task family:
definition drift, grain mismatch, hidden filters, timezone boundaries, fan-out joins,
failure-to-zero coercion, duplicated semantic logic, and upstream renames whose BI blast
radius is not visible from the warehouse. Several sources also show that the correct
first step is requirements and usage discovery rather than writing SQL.

The main gaps are first-party incident reports from Tableau, Power BI, Looker, Hex, and
Sigma operators; quantified frequencies for analytics work; and robust evidence for
dashboard administration workflows beyond migration and deprecation.
